In Development

Sin Francisco

View All Joint Projects →

A prestige frontier crime saga set in Gold Rush–era San Francisco. Three intersecting lives. A city still deciding what it wants to be — built on stolen land, slave capital, and the bones of those it consumed to get there.

Sin Francisco charts the nefarious birth of a city across colliding lives — a brutal reckoning in which legacy and vengeance ignite a Gold Rush Babylon that devours the weak, crowns the ruthless, and demands, sooner or later, that every sin be named.

A prestige frontier crime saga about power, race, and America's original sin.

// Services

What we
offer.

I.
Screenwriting

Original scripts developed from concept to completion. Feature, pilot, short — we write across formats with the same commitment to craft.

II.
Script Editing

Structural and line-level editing for scripts at any stage. We find what isn't working and tell you plainly — then help you fix it.

III.
Proofreading

Meticulous proofreading in correct screenplay format. Industry-standard presentation, every time.

IV.
Script Analysis

Detailed coverage and script reports. Honest, professional assessments that give writers actionable insight, not empty encouragement.

V.
Development

Story development and consultation for projects at the idea or outline stage. We help shape material before a word of script is written.

VI.
Collaboration

Open to co-writing engagements and creative partnerships on the right projects. Bring us your world — we'll help you build it.

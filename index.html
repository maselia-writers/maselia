We write worlds
others inhabit.

Maselia is a collective of three working screenwriters united by a shared conviction: that great stories demand rigour, craft, and a willingness to go where it's uncomfortable.

We work independently on our own projects and together on joint endeavours — bringing the full weight of three perspectives to bear on material that deserves more than one pair of eyes.

Available for commissions, collaborations, and professional script services across all stages of development.

// 004 — Services

What we
offer.

I.
Screenwriting

Original scripts developed from concept to completion. Feature, pilot, short — across formats with the same commitment to craft.

II.
Script Editing

Structural and line-level editing for scripts at any stage. We find what isn't working and tell you plainly — then help you fix it.

III.
Proofreading

Meticulous proofreading in correct screenplay format. Industry-standard presentation, every time.

IV.
Script Analysis

Detailed coverage and reports. Honest assessments that give writers actionable insight, not empty encouragement.

V.
Development

Story development and consultation at the idea or outline stage. We help shape material before a word of script is written.

VI.
Collaboration

Open to co-writing engagements and creative partnerships on the right projects. Bring us your world — we'll help you build it.

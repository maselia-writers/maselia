ChrisGibson

Screenwriter · Poet · Greece

A lifelong devotee of emotive expression, Chris has spent two decades honing his voice through short fiction and poetry, exploring the precision of language and emotional resonance. These years weren't merely practice; they were an apprenticeship in understanding what makes a story universal, what makes a moment unforgettable and what makes an audience lean forward in their seats. Now, Chris channels this wealth of experience into screenwriting, where depth meets immediacy, with his first script placing 2nd at the Hollywood Jumbo TV Pilot Screenplay Competition (2025).

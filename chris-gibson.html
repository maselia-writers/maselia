ChrisGibson

Screenwriter · Greece

Chris, whose devotion to the literary arts has constituted the better part of two decades, has applied himself to the crafts of short fiction and verse with the particular discipline of a man who understands that a writer in possession of nothing but time and an honest sentence is in possession of rather more than most — these years having served not as idle experiment but as a rigorous apprenticeship in the fundamental questions of the form: what renders a story universal in its appeal, what elevates a single moment above the merely competent and into the genuinely unforgettable and what arrangement of words, incident and human truth causes an audience to abandon the comfortable recline of their seats and incline, with involuntary and gratifying urgency, toward the stage — all of which considerable preparation Mr. Chris has now brought to bear upon the art of the screenplay the result of which has been recognized by no less an authority than the Hollywood Jumbo Television Pilot Screenplay Competition (2025) and from which this industry may reasonably expect to hear a great deal more.

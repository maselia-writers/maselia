Screenwriter · Content Creator · Bulgaria

Michael Cotter is a screenwriter and content creator with a background in computational biology and computing — a rare convergence that informs work of unusual structural rigour and thematic depth. Contributing writer on The Renaissance of Oddity Todd (2026) and co-lead writer on an original television pilot in active development.
